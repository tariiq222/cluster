# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Cluster primarily serves health-cluster operations administrators working within role- and scope-constrained contexts to manage organization structures, workflows, documents, reporting, and platform operations. All authenticated cluster employees are a secondary audience, using the same workspace for personal tasks, notifications, documents, requests, and scope-aware work. On a typical screen, the user must be able to review the current state, identify the permitted next action, and complete it confidently.

## Product Purpose

Cluster unifies fragmented health-cluster administrative work in one permission-aware workspace. It brings daily coordination, organizational administration, governed records, reporting, and platform operations into coherent workflows. Success means users can complete high-stakes administrative work correctly, without ambiguity about state, scope, ownership, or the next action.

## Positioning

Governed work, one place: administrative work stays clear, permission-correct, and traceable in one bilingual system.

## Brand Personality

Calm, authoritative, and precise. The voice is direct and exact without becoming stiff, and the interface remains focused without visual noise. Learn from Stripe Dashboard’s dense information hierarchy and predictable tables and settings, and from Linear’s fast navigation, restrained controls, and efficient interaction.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic template UI: a recognizable shadcn demo assembled without Cluster-specific hierarchy, workflow judgment, or operational context.

## Design Principles

1. **Begin with state and scope.** Make the current state, ownership, effective scope, and available next action understandable before asking the user to act.
2. **Optimize for correct completion.** Reduce ambiguity at decision points and preserve entered work through validation, conflicts, and recoverable errors.
3. **Make governance clarifying.** Permissions, approvals, versions, and auditability should explain what can happen next rather than feel like hidden friction.
4. **Use calm, familiar density.** Support complex administration with predictable controls, disciplined hierarchy, and fast navigation instead of decoration or novelty.
5. **Preserve bilingual parity.** Arabic-first RTL and English LTR experiences must remain equivalent in meaning, capability, and interaction quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 Level AA is the required baseline. Arabic-first RTL and equivalent English LTR content and interaction behavior must remain in parity.
